INTRODUCTION

I am a PhD-trained engineer specializing in computational speech neuroscience. I earned my Ph.D. in Bioengineering from the University of California, Berkeley and the University of California, San Francisco, where I developed computational models of speech motor control, applied Bayesian inference to human behavioral data, and designed studies of speech motor learning. My work bridges engineering, neuroscience, and data science, with experience spanning academic research, industry engineering, teaching, and mentorship. I am passionate about developing technologies for speech rehabilitation and diagnosis, monitoring, and treatment of neurological and psychiatric disorders.
